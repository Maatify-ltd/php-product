# Code of Conduct — maatify/php-product

[![Maatify PHP Product](https://img.shields.io/badge/Maatify-PHP_Product-blue?style=for-the-badge)](https://github.com/Maatify-ltd/php-product)
[![Maatify Ecosystem](https://img.shields.io/badge/Maatify-Ecosystem-9C27B0?style=for-the-badge)](https://github.com/Maatify)

Treat contributors and maintainers with respect. Discuss technical disagreements constructively, give specific feedback, and respect other people's privacy. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, personal attacks, and sharing private information without consent are unacceptable.

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, and communications where someone represents the project.

Report conduct concerns privately to [support@maatify.dev](mailto:support@maatify.dev). Maintainers review reports and may request changes, remove content, or restrict participation depending on the conduct.
